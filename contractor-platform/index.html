Predicted delays — flagged before they land

The platform forecasts each work package from live progress and dependency slip, flagging schedule breaches ahead of time so directives can be issued while there is still recovery runway.

Live activity — humans & robots

Contractor roster — human & robotic, interchangeable

Every work package can be delivered by a human crew or an autonomous fleet. Contractors are scored on the same axes, so the platform — and you — can swap one for the other on capability, price, and schedule fit.

Open tenders — pick the best proposal

Each open package draws competing bids from human and robotic contractors. The platform scores them on cost, duration, delivery risk, availability and rating, and recommends one — you award with a click, and the timeline re-plans around the winner.

Agentic control loop.
The platform detects a schedule breach, drafts a corrective directive, and — on your approval — issues it to the contractor over the right channel: a human crew receives an instruction via Procore/RFI; an autonomous fleet is dispatched a machine command via the Trimble / fleet MCP connector, which acknowledges and begins executing automatically.

Connected systems — MCP

The platform speaks to the tools heavy-industry programmes already run on, through Model Context Protocol servers. Primavera P6 is the schedule of record; progress flows in, and directives and re-baselines flow back out.
