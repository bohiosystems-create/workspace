KINAN · EstateMaster agent (analyst)

Reads EstateMaster. Prepares the analyst’s workbook.

What it is

Same reading, checking and reporting as the read-only agent. In addition, an approved change becomes a task with a control workbook for the analyst: the change, where it goes in EstateMaster, and the figures expected after it. The analyst enters it in EstateMaster and exports again; the agent then reconciles.

What the agent refuses, and why

Writing into EstateMasterThis agent never touches EstateMaster. It prepares the workbook; a person enters the change and exports.
Assuming a missing inputNo land cost, finance rate or cash flow is ever assumed; the chat asks.
Showing an IRR it cannot reproduceThe base IRR must be reproduced from the export’s own cash flow before any IRR change is computed.
Reporting an IRR where none exists“No IRR exists” is a possible answer.
Closing a task without an exportA task stays open until a new export is uploaded and reconciles with the expected figures, or a person marks it done.

Where the figures come from

Three kinds of figure appear, always labelled: EstateMaster’s (read from an export or the API, never recalculated), exact (computed by the agent from EstateMaster’s own cash flow, proved against EstateMaster’s IRR first) and AI estimate (two models, cross-checked; shown only when nothing exact is possible). Click any bold figure to see its working.
