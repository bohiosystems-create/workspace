BOHIOAI Assistant Director of Marketing

An AI assistant director of marketing for a one-person marketing function

Built for a real-estate developer whose CRM is Kinan's (Yardi plus Kinan's AI agent). It holds the plan to the sales targets, runs the marketing vendors, proves what they deliver, decides where the money goes and tells the manager every morning which campaigns to change. Leads, follow-up and sales stay with Kinan's own agent; the director reads the CRM results. The manager makes the decisions; the director does the rest.

Ground rule. Nothing that spends money or contacts a vendor or a customer happens without a named person approving it. Every approval is in the audit trail.
Director · home

Plan and decide

  • Daily brief: sales vs target (CRM-verified), the project furthest behind, next month's forecast, risks, this week's actions
  • Campaign recommendations in the brief: today's daily check first, then pause or shift budget, campaigns not converting, Meta and tracking issues, tests — each with the reason and one action
  • Budget plan that moves money to the vendors bringing the most extra sales per riyal, within each vendor's renewal range
  • One approval inbox with a time estimate per decision and a weekly total
  • Campaign quality from the CRM: strongest, middle, weakest
Daily check

What to change in each campaign, every morning

  • Each live campaign checked against its own trend and similar past campaigns: cost to sales vs history, rising cost per qualified lead, falling quality or volume, pacing, winners to scale, summer ahead, ending soon, stale CRM feed
  • Evidence, one action and the lessons of similar past campaigns per item
  • New today, open since, resolved since yesterday; accept or dismiss, and the decision carries over
  • Optional AI second opinion (Claude or OpenAI)
Ideas

Campaign ideation

  • Ideas from a brief (or none): grounded in the target gap, season, history lessons, today's checks and available vendors
  • Channel mix, vendors, forecast ranges computed from the history, guardrails and measurement
  • With AI: two models propose, Claude ranks against the data; approving drafts a vendor brief
History

2023–2025 campaigns

  • 43 past campaigns (2023–2025): launches, Ramadan, summer, always-on, events and Cityscape, brand, radio, billboards — including Palm Villas, sold out in 2024
  • Benchmarks by channel, season, year, project and vendor; a lesson per campaign
  • The yardstick for the daily check and the assistant
Orchestration

Runs the vendors

  • Vendor directory: open any vendor for its campaigns, Oracle invoices, work orders, deliverables and Outlook emails
  • Monthly briefs from the approved plan: budget, campaign codes, targets, deliverables and due dates
  • Monthly lead feedback from the CRM: what converted, why leads were lost
  • Two reminders for late work, then it asks the manager to call
  • Non-renewal notices with a handover list
  • Checks results against the data and closes the work order
Reports

Daily scheduled report

  • Every scheduled morning (default 07:30 Riyadh, Sun–Thu, English and Arabic)
  • Shows what changed since the last report, and the campaign recommendations with what's at stake
  • Emailed via Outlook to internal addresses only; full history to view or download
Performance

Measured fairly

  • Spend → leads → qualified → viewings → reservations → contracts → sales
  • Vendor-reported vs verified numbers (ad platforms, CRM, Oracle)
  • Scorecard against each channel's benchmark, adjusted for budget, with a range and confidence
Meta ads

Which agency runs each campaign

  • Reads Meta ad accounts and campaigns: spend, leads, tracking codes, who created each one
  • Works out the agency from the code in the name, utm_campaign, the creator and the account owner, with a confidence level
  • Flags agencies that aren't your vendors, conflicting evidence, campaigns without tracking codes, and your own boosted posts
  • You confirm or correct in one click
Incrementality

Proof of what a vendor caused

  • Holdout and geo tests with lift, interval and cost per extra result
  • Test designer with minimum detectable lift; starts only on approval
  • Media-mix model with seasonality (Ramadan, summer)
Decisions

Renew or replace

  • Re-engage, renegotiate, performance plan, test a replacement or exit, with evidence and confidence
  • Quarterly reviews, billing anomaly checks, replacement RFPs
  • Bench of alternatives, paid trials read from the CRM, one-click promotion
Invoices

Supplier invoices (Oracle)

  • Invoices reconciled with delivery: variances, duplicates, no PO, overruns, overdue payments
  • Approve or dispute; critical exceptions cannot be approved; nothing written back to Oracle
Assistant

Ask in English or Arabic

  • Answers on targets, the daily check, any campaign (live or past), vendors, projects, channels, any month, quarter or year, comparisons, history and lessons, metric definitions, tests, invoices, Meta
  • Claude, OpenAI and Gemini built in, with a task router (best provider per task, automatic fallback) and 20 read-only data tools
  • Charts from a prompt: "pie chart of revenue by vendor", "monthly spend as a line", with numbers computed from the data; switch the type and download as PNG or SVG
  • Without a key: built-in answers, checked by a 145-question English/Arabic test plus 148 off-script phrasings
  • Testable in the Claude app: Claude answers through your own account, no key
  • Drafts vendor emails in the vendor's language, sent only after approval
Kinan

CRM: Yardi + Kinan's AI agent

  • Kinan's agent owns leads, follow-up and sales; the director reads the CRM results
  • Shares the approved plan, campaign codes and changes, and the daily brief; signed and retried
  • Whole app, emails and reports in Arabic, right to left

Before the day

Say this upfront

  • Sample data, frozen on 8 June 2026. Reports are dated today and say "Figures as of 8 Jun 2026".
  • Integrations are simulated. Outlook, Kinan, Yardi and Oracle are in mock mode, and the screens say so. Nothing is sent.
  • One manager, no team. The director does the team's work; you only approve.

Storyline

Question bank

All tested. In the demo file the assistant uses built-in answers, which cover a wide range; these are safe bets.

Pitfalls

  • Reloading the demo file resets everything. Don't reload mid-demo. To reset the live app: Ctrl+C, then npm run demo:live.
  • Buttons greyed out? Your name is missing in "Approving as", or the "I have read" box isn't ticked.
  • Off-script question? Say: "With Claude or OpenAI connected it answers anything from this data."
  • CRM-feed alert is expected: the sample CRM data ends on 30 May; the clock is 8 June.

Questions to ask the client

  • Which Yardi product and version does Kinan run, and can we write activities and marketing sources into it?
  • Where is the campaign code captured on a lead in Yardi?
  • Does Kinan's agent want the plan, campaign changes and daily brief, and through what (webhook, queue, MCP)?
  • What are the real monthly sales targets per project?
  • Vendor list, contract end dates and notice periods, account-manager emails.
  • Oracle supplier numbers and access; report recipients and preferred time.
  • Meta: which ad accounts exist, who owns each, which agencies have partner access, and will agencies adopt the naming and utm_campaign convention?
