# NCR-0089 — Reinforcement cover below specification, Tower A Level 13 columns C-7 and C-8

**Project:** Kinan Heights · **Raised by:** Resident Engineer (client's supervision consultant) · **Against:** Al-Bunyan Contracting (Main Contractor) · **Date:** 13 October 2026 · **Location:** Tower A, Level 13, grid C/7 and C/8 · **Severity:** Major · **Status:** Open · **Due:** 27 October 2026

## Non-conformance
Cover-meter survey after striking the column formwork on Level 13 measured 22–27 mm cover to the links on the east faces of columns C-7 and C-8, against **40 mm specified** (Concrete Specification 03 30 00 §3.4, exposure XC4). Six readings per face, calibrated Profometer, survey sheet CS-TA-L13-02. Columns are C60, cast 9 October 2026 (pour card PC-TA-13-0412). Likely cause: spacers displaced during the pour, column cages not tied to the kicker.

## Disposition proposed by the contractor
Apply a 15 mm polymer-modified cementitious render (Sika MonoTop-412 or equal) to the affected faces after surface preparation, in line with the repair method statement MS-REP-004, and increase spacer frequency to 600 mm on all tower columns. Structural adequacy unaffected (cover is a durability requirement).

## Engineer's decision
Pending: a core sample to confirm actual cover and compaction; then accept the render repair or require removal. Hold on TA-STR-L14 column pours at C-7 and C-8 until spacer arrangement is inspected (ITR-0412).
