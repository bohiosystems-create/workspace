# Safety Observation OBS-0338 — Edge protection removed and not reinstated, Tower B Level 31 (north edge)

**Observed by:** Tariq Aziz (HSE Officer, night shift) · **Date/time:** 13 October 2026, 23:20 · **Location:** Tower B, Level 31, north slab edge between grids B/2 and B/5 (about 18 m) · **Category:** Unsafe condition — fall from height · **Risk:** High · **Status:** Open, immediate action taken

## What was observed
A section of the slab-edge guardrail had been removed to land the façade unit cassette by crane TC2 and was not reinstated after the lift. Two formwork carpenters were working within 2 m of the open edge without harnesses clipped on. This is the same level as near miss INC-2026-041 (unsecured plywood, 10 October 2026).

## Immediate action
Work stopped, the two workers moved back, guardrail reinstated by 23:45, toolbox talk given at the start of the next shift (28 attendees).

## Root cause (preliminary)
No permit or barrier-removal tag was used for the lift; the façade lifting supervisor and the formwork foreman did not hand over the edge. The lifting plan for façade cassettes does not include guardrail reinstatement as a step.

## Actions
1. Add "guardrail reinstated and checked" as a sign-off step on the façade lifting plan (Bilal Khan, Lifting Supervisor) — due 16 October 2026.
2. Barrier-removal tags issued to all lifting supervisors (Sanjay Menon) — due 15 October 2026.
3. Weekly night-shift edge inspection on all live floors of Tower A and B (Tariq Aziz) — ongoing.
